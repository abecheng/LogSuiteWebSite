From exam room to SOAP — without opening the EHR.

ScribeLog is a personal clinical memo tool, not a medical record system. Capture first. Structure when you have a minute.

  1. 01

    Record

    Audio lands on-device the moment you stop. Crash, call, or kill the app — the recording is already saved.

  2. 02

    Transcribe

    Speech turns into text in the background. Review later. Edit when it matters. Choose the language you speak.

  3. 03

    File & follow up

    Assign visits to a patient. Fill S/O/A/P by hand or with optional AI. Add follow-ups and to-dos — sync to Apple Calendar and Reminders when you want.

What you get day to day.

Everything below is built around one idea: each patient holds the visits, SOAP, dates, and follow-ups you need between sessions — without a heavy EHR.

Patients, Inbox & Visits

Dump a voice note into Inbox, then file it under the right patient. Review transcripts, SOAP, and edits on your schedule.

SOAP on every visit

Subjective, Objective, Assessment, Plan — hand-filled offline, or drafted from a transcript with your own AI key. You confirm before anything is saved.

Follow-ups & to-dos

Pin return visits and dated tasks on the patient. ScribeLog can write them to Apple Calendar and Reminders when you turn sync on — and remove its copies when you turn sync off.

Search across the chart

Find patients, transcripts, SOAP fields, schedule items, and to-dos from Today. Open results straight into the right patient or visit.

Export PDF & private backup

Share a printable PDF with the sections you check — never dump full voice transcripts by accident. Or pack a private zip of visits (metadata, audio) for your own archive.

Home Screen widgets (iPhone)

Due Today for today’s follow-ups and tasks. Record to jump straight into a new voice memo — including a Lock Screen option.

Optional BYOK AI

Bring your own API key. Ask AI to draft SOAP, a summary, follow-ups, and to-dos from a transcript — then edit and confirm before anything is saved. Keys stay on your device. Create a key via Gemini docs or OpenRouter.

iPhone & iPad

Same patient book across your Apple devices. Text syncs via your iCloud private database when available; recordings live in iCloud Drive folders you can see in Files.

Private by default.

No ScribeLog account. No ScribeLog-operated clinical server. Notes are saved on your device first. When iCloud is available, structured text can sync through your iCloud private database, and audio can use iCloud Documents — so you stay in control. Optional AI calls go only to the provider you configure — with your own key. ScribeLog is a personal sidecar chart, not a covered entity EHR.
