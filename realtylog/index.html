From the curb to a filed note — without a brokerage CRM.

RealtyLog is a personal showing sidecar for solo buyer’s agents (you can list too). Capture first. Structure when you have a minute.

  1. 01

    Record

    Audio lands on-device the moment you stop. Crash, call, or kill the app — the recording is already saved.

  2. 02

    Transcribe

    Speech turns into text in the background. Review later. Edit when it matters. Choose the language you speak.

  3. 03

    File & follow

    Assign notes to a buyer and a listing. Keep shared house condition on the property; keep love / maybe / pass on the showing. Sync follow-ups to Apple Calendar and Reminders when you want.

What you get between showings.

Built for the driveway dump: each note can hang on a buyer, a seller, or a property — without becoming MLS, transaction management, or a licensed inspection app.

Buyers, Sellers & Properties

Three lines of work, one data model. Match inventory to a buyer from Properties; open the same listing from the seller’s card. Showings stay bidirectional.

Condition on the house

Exterior, interior, systems, neighborhood, and red flags live on the property — shared across visits. Buyer pulse, intent, and next step stay on that day’s showing.

Today, schedule & to-dos

Due items for offers, second looks, and listing prep. Optional sync to Apple Calendar and Reminders — remove RealtyLog copies when you turn sync off.

Search across the book

Find clients, transcripts, condition notes, reminders, and file names from Today. Open results straight into the right person or listing.

Export PDF & private backup

Light summaries for free paths; fuller property work-papers when you go Pro — framed as advisor memos, not certified home inspections. Or pack a private zip for your own archive.

Light map on the listing

Pin an address, open Apple Maps, get directions from the property Info sheet. No map-based home search, heat maps, or listing discovery.

Optional BYOK AI

Bring your own API key. Ask AI to draft condition blocks and follow-ups from a transcript — then edit and confirm before anything is saved. Keys stay on your device. Create a key via Gemini docs or OpenRouter.

iPhone & iPad

Same book across your Apple devices. Text syncs via your iCloud private database when available; recordings live in iCloud Drive folders you can see in Files.

Private by default.

No RealtyLog account. No RealtyLog-operated business server. Notes are saved on your device first. When iCloud is available, structured text can sync through your iCloud private database, and audio can use iCloud Documents — so you stay in control. Optional AI calls go only to the provider you configure — with your own key. RealtyLog is a personal showing sidecar, not MLS, brokerage CRM, or a licensed home inspection product.
