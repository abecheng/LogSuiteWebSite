From the lane to a bid note — without a 200-checkbox platform.

AutoLog is a personal inspection memo tool, not a certified multi-point report system. Capture first. Structure when you have a minute.

  1. 01

    Record

    Audio lands on-device the moment you stop. Crash, call, or kill the app — the recording is already saved.

  2. 02

    Transcribe

    Speech turns into text in the background. Review later. Edit when it matters. Choose the language you speak.

  3. 03

    File & bid

    Assign notes to a vehicle. Fill VIF and critical screens by hand or with optional AI. Add recon costs and Max Bid — sync follow-ups to Apple Calendar and Reminders when you want.

What you get on the lot.

Built for 60-second to 2-minute auction looks: each vehicle holds inspections, VIF, recon dollars, and evidence — without a heavy dealer DMS or platform checklist.

Vehicles, Inbox & Inspections

Dump a voice note into Inbox, then file it under the right vehicle. Review transcripts, VIF, and edits on your schedule.

VIF on every look

Exterior, mechanical, interior, road test — hand-filled offline, or drafted from a transcript with your own AI key. Critical screens (structural / flood / fire): PASS, ATTENTION, or N/A — never a silent Pass. You confirm before anything is saved.

Recon $ & Max Bid

Line items with estimated cost and Total Recon. Pin Max Bid and dated follow-ups on the vehicle. Sync to Apple Calendar and Reminders when you turn it on — and remove AutoLog copies when you turn it off.

Search across the lot

Find vehicles, transcripts, VIF, recon, and file names from Today. Open results straight into the right vehicle or inspection.

Export PDF & private backup

Free light PDF for identity and a short summary. Pro full VIF PDF with critical screens, recon $, bid worksheet, album, and disclaimer. Or pack a private zip for your own archive — never dump full voice by accident unless you choose to.

Home Screen widgets (iPhone)

Due Today for recon follow-ups. Record to jump straight into a new voice memo — including a Lock Screen option.

Optional BYOK AI

Bring your own API key. Ask AI to draft VIF, critical screens, recon lines with $, and Max Bid — then edit and confirm before anything is saved. Keys stay on your device. Create a key via Gemini docs or OpenRouter.

iPhone & iPad

Same vehicle book across your Apple devices. Text syncs via your iCloud private database when available; recordings live in iCloud Drive folders you can see in Files.

Private by default.

No AutoLog account. No AutoLog-operated business server. Notes are saved on your device first. When iCloud is available, structured text can sync through your iCloud private database, and audio can use iCloud Documents — so you stay in control. Optional AI calls go only to the provider you configure — with your own key. AutoLog is a personal inspection sidecar, not a certified platform report or vehicle history product.
