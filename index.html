From hallway to hearing — without opening a practice suite.

MatterLog is a personal memo tool, not a firm OS. Capture first. Organize when you have a minute.

  1. 01

    Record

    Audio lands on-device the moment you stop. Crash, call, or kill the app — the recording is already saved.

  2. 02

    Transcribe

    Speech turns into text in the background. Review later. Edit when it matters. Choose the language you speak.

  3. 03

    File & follow up

    Assign notes to a matter. Add schedule, to-dos, and case files. Optionally sync deadlines to Apple Calendar and Reminders.

What you get day to day.

Everything below is built around one idea: each matter holds the memos, dates, files, and follow-ups you need between courts — without a heavy practice-management suite.

Matters, Inbox & Review

Dump a voice note into Inbox, then file it under the right matter. Review transcripts, summaries, and edits on your schedule.

Schedule & to-dos

Pin hearings and dated tasks on the matter. MatterLog can write them to Apple Calendar and Reminders when you turn sync on — and remove its copies when you turn sync off.

Case files

Attach PDFs, images, and common office documents to a matter. Preview, rename, share, or save out — video and installers stay blocked on purpose.

Search across the casebook

Find matters, transcripts, schedule items, to-dos, and file names from Today. Open results straight into the right matter tab.

Export PDF & private backup

Share a printable PDF with only the sections you check — never full voice transcripts. Or pack a private zip of the whole matter (metadata, audio, attachments) for your own archive.

Home Screen widgets (iPhone)

Due Today for today’s hearings and tasks. Record to jump straight into a new voice memo — including a Lock Screen option.

Optional AI organize

Bring your own API key. Ask AI to draft a summary, fields, schedule, and to-dos from a transcript — then edit and confirm before anything is saved. Keys stay on your device.

iPhone, iPad & Mac

Same matter book across Apple devices. Text syncs via your iCloud private database when available; recordings and attachments live in iCloud Drive folders you can see in Files / Finder.

Private by default.

No MatterLog account. No MatterLog-operated case server. Notes are saved on your device first. When iCloud is available, structured text can sync through your iCloud private database, and audio and attachments can use iCloud Documents — so you stay in control. Optional AI calls go only to the provider you configure — with your own key.
