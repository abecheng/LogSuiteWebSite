What you can count on 你可以放心的地方

Simple promises — no jargon required. 几件简单的事，说清楚就好。

  • Private by default 默认保护隐私 Your notes live on your phone. Sync only if you choose — we don’t ask you to create an account with us. 备忘留在你的手机里。要不要同步由你决定——不用在我们这边注册账号。
  • Fast when you’re busy 忙碌时也够快 Open, speak, done. Built for the gap between one client and the next — not for long desk sessions. 打开、开口、完成。专为「下一个客户还在等」的空隙设计，不是坐在桌前慢慢填表。
  • Speaks your language 懂你的行话 Each app uses the words of your field — matters, patients, listings, vehicles — so it feels familiar from day one. 每个 App 用你行业的说法——案件、患者、房源、车辆——打开就知道怎么用。
